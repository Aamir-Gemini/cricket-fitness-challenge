    About This Challenge

    This 15-day program is designed specifically to build the **functional strength, core stability, and endurance** needed for competitive cricket.

    Warm-up (8 Minutes)

    Dynamic movements to elevate heart rate and increase blood flow.

    Stretching & Mobilization (7 Minutes)

    Focus on dynamic stretching and joint mobility relevant to the day's WOD.

    Workout of the Day (WOD - 40 Minutes)

    The high-intensity functional training section, using various formats (EMOM, AMRAP, Rounds for Time).

    Cool-down & Static Stretching (5 Minutes)

    Static holds to reduce heart rate and improve flexibility.
